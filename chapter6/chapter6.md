At the end of the last chapter, our stack could finally accept connections on the ports an application asked for, but the `TcpStream`s it handed out were empty shells: you could hold one, but you couldn't say anything over it. In this chapter, we'll implement Rust's `Read` and `Write` traits for `TcpStream`, put the `incoming` and `unacked` queues to work, and send our first bytes of real data over a TCP connection we built ourselves.
